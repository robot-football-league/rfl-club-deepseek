# DeepSeek Rovers — identity prompts for the league renderer

Every asset should share the club palette: deep blue `#1220BF`,
electric blue `#4D6BFC`, ice white `#F0F2FF`, and a single accent line
of match-white. The club mascot is DeepSeek's whale, rendered as a
sweeping origami-like crest line rather than a cartoon.

---

## Badge (circular crest, on transparent background)

A circular football crest, flat vector style. A deep-blue disc
(#1220BF) with a thin ice-white inner ring. Centered on the disc, a
single continuous white line drawing of a leaping whale, folded from
angular planes like origami, its tail swept behind it and a small
five-pointed star in the negative space above its back. Beneath the
whale, the club wordmark "DEEPSEEK ROVERS" in bold condensed
sans-serif caps, white, arced along the bottom inside the ring. Above
the whale, the three-letter code "DSK" in small white letters. No
photographic texture, no gradients, crisp geometric shapes, 1:1
square, suitable for a 256 px icon.

## Home kit (deep blue)

A football kit flat-lay: jersey, shorts and socks on a neutral
grey-green background. Jersey is deep blue (#1220BF) with electric
blue (#4D6BFC) shoulder panels and a single ice-white horizontal
stripe across the chest. The badge sits on the left chest; the
goalkeeper-style round collar is ice white. Shorts are deep blue with
a thin electric-blue stripe down each side. Socks are deep blue with
two ice-white bands at the top. Fabric should look technical, matte,
no photoreal player, no sponsor text, 2:3 portrait.

---

## Away kit (ice white)

A football kit flat-lay: jersey, shorts and socks on a neutral
mid-grey background. Jersey is ice white (#F0F2FF) with deep blue
(#1220BF) raglan sleeves and a single electric-blue (#4D6BFC)
horizontal chest band. The badge sits on the left chest; the round
collar and sleeve cuffs are deep blue. Shorts are ice white with a
thin deep-blue stripe down each side. Socks are ice white with two
deep-blue bands at the top. Fabric should look technical, matte, no
photoreal player, no sponsor text, 2:3 portrait. Must read clearly as
the light kit against the dark home kit at a glance.
